Hi, here's what we have for the plexiform neurofibroma RNA-seq set we want to share.

Four patients, all NF1, ages 14, 17, 22 and 31. Biopsies taken during debulking
surgery, flash-frozen in theatre. Human samples, primary tumour tissue.

Library prep was done in the core: poly-A selected, KAPA mRNA HyperPrep kit.
Sequenced on a NovaSeq 6000, paired-end 2x150. The core returned FASTQs, two per
sample, plus RIN values from the Bioanalyzer.

We also tracked how long each sample sat between removal and freezing, which the
pathology group asked us to record. That's the cold ischemia column.

Let me know what else you need before we can publish this.
